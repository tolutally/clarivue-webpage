About Clarivue

Clarivue AI is developed and published by SkillConnect Technologies Inc., a Canadian company registered federally in Canada. We are building Clarivue AI to change how interviews are run and decisions are made. Our team cares about one thing: making hiring faster, fairer, and backed by evidence.

Our Mission

We believe interviews decide futures, and they deserve more than gut feeling. Clarivue was created to give hiring teams and agencies structure, data, and speed—without losing the human touch. We guide every conversation with role-based prompts, turn job descriptions into clear scorecards, capture evidence as you go, and help teams decide faster with an auditable trail of what was asked, said, and scored. The result: fairer interviews, clearer feedback, and confident decisions made in hours, not weeks.

How We Work

We blend AI guidance with practical workflows. The result is interviews that stay on track, scorecards that complete themselves, and decisions that land in inboxes with proof attached.

Our Values

Fair interviews. Less bias. Faster yes or no. Transparency for teams and candidates alike.

Our Team

Recruiters, technologists, and builders working side by side. We know the pain of messy interviews, and we're here to fix it.

Our Process

Listen first, then build. Every feature is tested with hiring managers, recruiters, and students to ensure it solves a real pain point before it ships.

Our Clients

From startups to staffing agencies to career offices, we work with teams that want to move faster without losing quality.

Our Impact

We cut wasted hours, reduce manual admin, and give teams the confidence to make hiring decisions backed by data.

Our Vision

A hiring world where every interview is structured, every decision is clear, and talent moves forward without delays.
